# 🏡 TinyQuest: Adventures at Grandma's House

## 🌟 Game Overview

**TinyQuest** is a heartwarming, child-friendly exploration and hidden-object game built in **C++ for Unreal Engine 5.4**. Designed specifically for young minds, the game replaces stressful mechanics and time limits with a gentle, encouraging experience focused on **curiosity, discovery, kindness, and helping others**.

Players can freely explore a cozy countryside setting, uncover hidden treasures, and assist friendly neighbors while enjoying a safe and relaxing adventure.

---

## 📖 Story

Little **<Player Name>** (or a custom character) is spending the weekend at **Grandma's cozy country house**. Surrounded by colorful gardens, charming neighbors, and fascinating old trinkets, the house is full of secrets waiting to be discovered.

But there's a small problem—Grandma and her friendly neighbors are a bit forgetful!

As the player explores the house, backyard, and nearby neighborhood, they will meet local children and townsfolk who need help finding misplaced toys, locating lost belongings, and solving simple mysteries. Along the way, players will uncover hidden treasures, learn about the world around them, and make new friends.

Every discovery brings a smile, every task helps someone in need, and every adventure makes Grandma's house feel even more like home.

---

## 🎮 Key Features

* 🌼 Relaxing, stress-free gameplay
* 🔍 Hidden-object and exploration-based adventures
* 🏡 Cozy country house and garden environments
* 🤝 Friendly NPCs with simple quests and requests
* 🎒 Collectibles, treasures, and secrets to discover
* 🎨 Bright, colorful visuals designed for children
* 📚 Positive themes of kindness, curiosity, and community
* 🎯 No combat, no failure states, and no time limits
